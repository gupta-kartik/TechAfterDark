# Speaker Guidelines

Thank you for speaking at Tech After Dark. These guidelines will help you prepare a great talk that fits the format and energy of the event.

---

## Format

- **Duration:** 10–15 minutes per talk
- **Style:** Conversational and practical — avoid heavy marketing language
- **Slides:** Keep slides minimal and visual; avoid walls of text
- **Demos:** Strongly encouraged where possible

---

## Audience

Attendees are typically:

- Enterprise developers and engineers
- Engineering managers
- DevOps and platform engineers
- Security engineers

Assume a **technical audience** who appreciates depth over broad overviews.

---

## Talk Topics

Speakers are typically assigned one of the following:

1. **Modern Software Development with GitHub**
   - GitHub Enterprise capabilities
   - Collaboration workflows
   - CI/CD with GitHub Actions

2. **AI-Assisted Development with GitHub Copilot**
   - Live demo strongly recommended
   - Focus on real developer productivity gains
   - Cover Copilot Chat, inline suggestions, and CLI if time allows

3. **Securing the Software Supply Chain**
   - GitHub Advanced Security
   - Code scanning, secret scanning, Dependabot
   - Practical examples of catching real vulnerabilities

---

## Preparation Checklist

- [ ] Slides submitted to event lead at least **3 days before** the event
- [ ] Demo environment tested and working
- [ ] Backup screenshots prepared in case of connectivity issues
- [ ] Talk timed and within 15 minutes

---

## On The Night

- Arrive by **[Venue open time]** for a speaker briefing
- You will be introduced by the MC
- Microphone will be provided
- Screen will be mirrored from your laptop via HDMI or AirPlay
- Questions from the audience will be taken at the discretion of the MC

---

## After The Event

Please share your final slides with the event lead so they can be distributed to attendees.
